# Meeting Templates

## Standup / Daily Sync

```markdown
# Daily Standup — [Date]

**Attendees:** [names]

## Updates

### [Person 1]
- Yesterday: [completed work]
- Today: [planned work]
- Blockers: [any blockers]

### [Person 2]
...

## Blockers Requiring Attention
- [List any blockers that need escalation]

## Quick Notes
[Any brief announcements]
```

## Team Meeting

```markdown
# [Meeting Title] — [Date]

**Attendees:** [names]  
**Facilitator:** [name]  
**Duration:** [time]

## Agenda
1. [Topic 1]
2. [Topic 2]
3. [Topic 3]

## Discussion

### [Topic 1]
[Summary of discussion]

**Decision:** [if any]

### [Topic 2]
[Summary of discussion]

**Decision:** [if any]

## Decisions Made
- [Decision 1]
- [Decision 2]

## Action Items
- [ ] @[owner]: [task] — [deadline]
- [ ] @[owner]: [task] — [deadline]

## Next Meeting
[Date/time if scheduled]
```

## Board / Formal Meeting

```markdown
# [Organization Name]
## [Meeting Type] Minutes — [Date]

**Location:** [location/virtual]  
**Called to Order:** [time]  
**Adjourned:** [time]

### Attendance
**Present:** [names]  
**Absent:** [names]  
**Guests:** [names]

### Approval of Previous Minutes
Motion to approve by [name], seconded by [name].  
**Vote:** Approved [unanimously / X-Y]

### Reports

#### [Report 1 Title]
Presented by [name].
[Summary]

### Old Business

#### [Item 1]
[Discussion summary]
**Motion:** [motion text]  
**Moved by:** [name] | **Seconded by:** [name]  
**Vote:** [result]

### New Business

#### [Item 1]
[Discussion summary]
**Motion:** [motion text]  
**Moved by:** [name] | **Seconded by:** [name]  
**Vote:** [result]

### Action Items
- [ ] [owner]: [task] — [deadline]

### Next Meeting
[Date, time, location]

---
**Minutes prepared by:** [name]  
**Approved by:** [name] on [date]
```

```markdown
**Attendees:** [names]  
**Facilitator:** [name]

## What Went Well 🎉
- [Item 1]
- [Item 2]
- [Item 3]

## What Could Improve 🔧
- [Item 1]
- [Item 2]
- [Item 3]

## Action Items for Next Sprint
- [ ] @[owner]: [improvement action] — [deadline]
- [ ] @[owner]: [improvement action] — [deadline]

## Shoutouts 🌟

## Metrics (if applicable)

- Carryover: [list]
```
